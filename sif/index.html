How to read this

  • Each return cell shows the return, then "/" and the volatility over the same period (standard deviation of daily returns scaled to the period length; annualised for periods longer than 1 year). The second line shows the fund’s quintile within its category (Q1 = top fifth) and a sparkline of its NAV over the period.
  • Returns up to 1 year are absolute. Any period longer than 1 year will be annualised.
  • Key stats: Sharpe and Sortino ratios from daily returns over the last 1 year, annualised, using a risk-free rate of 7%. NA for funds with less than 1 year of history.
  • WTD is measured from the last NAV of the previous week, MTD from the last NAV of the previous month. A fund launched within the period is measured from its launch price.
  • NA means the fund is too new for that period, or the figure is not yet reported.
  • AUM is the latest month-end figure reported by the fund house. Expense ratios are as of 31 Oct 2026.
  • Shading and the Q rank compare each fund only with funds in its own category, because hybrid and equity funds are not comparable. Categories with fewer than 5 funds reporting are not ranked. AUM is not shaded, since a bigger fund is not better or worse.
  • Click any column heading to sort. Click again to reverse.
